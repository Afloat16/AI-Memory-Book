## Starting point

Work starts from `861b7f35ac2557024d89bc51d5b3017ca35cb9d0`, the maintained
documentation state after v0.13 publication. Its runtime remains the accepted
v0.13 runtime; its historical source, release, scale evidence and frozen
protocols are unchanged. The user requested completion of remaining goals and
larger targeted experiments across long-lived, similar engineering projects.

Open goals recovered from the last checkpoint are archive-size-independent
access to fixed-size old projects, lower cold-recall cost without lost evidence,
clear handling of missing answers, and an independent real-source transfer test.
No guarantee of constant cost for every arbitrary large-project query follows
from a small-project optimization.

## Workstreams

- Performance: preserve global SQLite BM25 scoring while maintaining derived
  document frequencies and lengths incrementally. A complete small project can
  be scored from bounded project-local summaries; unsupported cases must fall
  back to the full original route. Transactional ingestion, deletion, source
  replacement, old writers and migration require regression coverage.
- Correctness: expose coverage of explicit identifiers in the actual returned
  excerpts, retaining related original text. Missing excerpts are insufficient
  evidence, not proof of global absence; a mention is not an authoritative answer.
- Longitudinal experiment: new v014 protocol, separate age/foreign/own-growth
  axes, interleaving, replacement, 200k-page main profile, exact source bindings,
  independent arm snapshots, cold/warm/first-connection/SQL-work observations.
- Transfer holdout: four real public upstream projects, eight pinned revisions,
  48 independently authored questions and original-byte/source-coordinate audits.

Protocol commits must precede formal queries. Keep every original failure and
attempt separate; never alter a question, gold span, denominator or old fixture
to hide a regression. Source and evidence commits remain distinguishable.
The candidate will be submitted on its own feature branch for review; this
work does not change the published v0.13.0 tag or its historical claims.
